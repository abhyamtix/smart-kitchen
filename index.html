About Smart Kitchen

Our goal is simple: less waste, smarter kitchens.

🎯 Project Objective

Smart Kitchen is designed to help users manage their kitchen inventory, monitor food freshness and make better use of ingredients before they expire.

✨ Current Features

✅ Manual food item entry
✅ Expiry date tracking
✅ Automatic freshness calculation
✅ Search and filtering
✅ Kitchen inventory management
✅ Recipe suggestions

🚀 Future Development

🔒 OCR food recognition
🔒 Image-based freshness detection
🔒 AI recipe recommendations
🔒 Smart expiry notifications
🔒 Grocery planning
🔒 Food waste analytics

👨‍💻 Project

Developed as a college mini project focused on combining web technology, artificial intelligence concepts and sustainable kitchen management.
